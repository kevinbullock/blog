***One year at DevSeed***

I’m stoked to be headed to DC to collaborate with the [Development Seed](https://developmentseed.org/) team for our annual team week. This is my second team week, as I just hit my one-year milestone! 

At the DevSeed kickoff, it’s organized, planned and run by our diverse team. Not corporate. Every team member has the opportunity to lead a session, organize an activity, orchestrate a workshop, or give a presentation based on topics they think are relevant and strategic. We don’t have lame corporate talking points being drilled into our heads, no Kool-Aid. It’s a week for the people, by the people. 

We’ll be talking about [GeoLLMs](https://developmentseed.org/blog/2025-01-29-llms/), GeoAI, Foundational Models, STAC and Cloud Native; just a few of the technologies DevSeed is cooking. The sessions I put on the calendar are a Disaster Response workshop, a trip to the Natural History Museum, and a 5k run. We’ll have a [Zine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zine) launch, a happy hour, a game night, rock climbing, and bird watching amongst other things. Yes, I do work at the coolest company, here’s why: 

- **We work in the Open:** We believe data and software have maximum impact when they are open and freely accessible. No proprietary licensing, no annual subscriptions to use the tools we’ve developed. To that end, we license our code under the Open MIT license and document everything.

- **Our team is kind, and brilliant:** for over a decade, I was always impressed with DevSeeders - their kindness, intellect, and empathy. I work with the highest caliber team, and everyday I am so impressed with their work and their leadership in software development. I’m humbled to be part of the team. 

- **Guided by a North Star:** DevSeed uses the “North Star” as a metaphor for the values that guide us as a company. The North Star is somewhat like a mission statement, that is frequently updated and guides us as an organization. It’s a beautiful analogy, as the celestial skies have guided navigation for [thousands of years](https://www.historyhit.com/how-celestial-navigation-changed-maritime-history/#:~:text=The%20art%20of%20celestial%20navigation,National%20Archaeological%20Museum%20of%20Athens.&text=The%20ancient%20Greeks%20also%20likely,3rd%20or%202nd%20century%20BC.). 

- **Nimble:** decisions at DevSeed are made fast, with purpose and intention, and backed by data. Quick decisions are only made in a high-trust organization, where everyone is encouraguge to chime in and provide insight. Importantly, every DevSeeder is empowered to make or lead decision making. One of our mottos is JFDI - Just Fuckin Do It.

- **Autonomy:** we aren’t beholden to shareholders or investors trying to maximize profit and hit quarterly financial milestones. Our work and our partners are the priority, and we selectively choose organizations to partner with that align with our values.

If you’re in DC next week, come say hi to me and meet the team at our [happy hour](https://lu.ma/e230t38z?tk=qbMnrz)!
